V111 - Shared Technician Assignment

- Removed individual "Assign Technician" selection from Engineer job creation.
- Every new Engineer/Admin-created job is assigned to all active approved technicians.
- All technicians keep seeing the shared job, including after another technician completes it.
- Completion remains per technician assignment; the UI shows the technician name(s) whose assignment is completed.
- Engineers now see the shared job pool instead of only their own created/assigned jobs.
- Existing Admin/Engineer/Technician job details, filters, dashboards and Extra Work are preserved.
